What data does this site collect?

This site does not use cookies, visitor statistics, advertising or tracking tools. The site has no server or database: what you enter in the forms is processed only in your own browser, is not stored by the site and is not sent anywhere by the site itself.

As a technical necessity, the hosting service on which the site is published may keep access logs of page requests (such as the IP address, date and time, and the page requested).

The service request form and WhatsApp

  • The information you fill in on the form (request type, equipment, urgency, area; optionally your name, phone number, machine and fault details, a description and, if you choose to share it, your location) is turned into a ready-made WhatsApp message when you press the "Create Request via WhatsApp" button.
  • When you press Send in WhatsApp, the message reaches our line {{TEL}}. Before sending, you can edit the message or decide not to send it.
  • WhatsApp is a service operated by Meta Platforms and may process messages under its own terms, including on servers outside Turkey. Using WhatsApp is your choice; you can also call us by phone.
  • The information that reaches us is used to assess your service request, to get back to you and to carry out the service you request.

Location data

  • Your location is taken only when you press the "Add my location" button on the service request page and grant the permission your browser asks for.
  • The location is added only to that message as a map link; the site does not store your location. You can remove it from the message with "Remove location".
  • Granting location permission is not required; you can type the address or directions yourself.

Google Maps

The map at the top of the home page is loaded from Google Maps when the page opens. Your browser then connects to Google; Google may process technical information such as your IP address under its own privacy terms, including on servers outside Turkey, and may use cookies. The maps on other pages are not loaded until you press the "Show map" button.

The "Get Directions", "Open in Google Maps" and "Share location" links also lead to Google Maps. "Share location" shares only the map link of our workshop; it does not collect your location.

Legal basis

The data you share at your request is processed under KVKK Article 5(2)(c) (being directly related to the conclusion or performance of a contract) and Article 5(2)(f) (legitimate interest, provided that your fundamental rights and freedoms are not harmed).

Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information if it has been processed; to learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose; to know the third parties to whom it has been transferred; to request its correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate; to request its erasure or destruction where the legal conditions are met; to request that these actions be notified to the third parties to whom the data has been transferred; to object to a result against you arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems; and to claim compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing.

You can send requests concerning these rights in writing to the address above.
