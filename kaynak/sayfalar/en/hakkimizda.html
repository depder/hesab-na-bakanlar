Service process

Six steps from request to handover.

  1. 01

    Send your request

    Fill in the service request cards, write on WhatsApp or call us. You can attach a photo of the faulty part and the machine's location.

  2. 02

    Initial assessment

    {{YETKILI}} reviews the information and photos you send and gets back to you.

  3. 03

    Workshop or on site

    The machine or part is brought to the workshop; if the machine is stuck on site, our team comes to it.

  4. 04

    Diagnosis and quote

    The fault is diagnosed; the scope of the work and the estimated time are confirmed.

  5. 05

    Repair and overhaul

    The work you approve is carried out.

  6. 06

    Inspection and handover

    The finished work is checked and handed over.
