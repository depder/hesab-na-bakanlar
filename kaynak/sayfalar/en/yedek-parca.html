Oils and fluids

The right oil for every compartment.

A construction machine does not take just one "oil". The engine, hydraulics, transmission, axles, final drives and swing reducer are separate compartments, and each needs an oil of a different grade and viscosity.

{{ikon:motor}}

Engine oil

Heavy-duty engine oil for diesel construction machinery. Newer engines with a diesel particulate filter (DPF) require low-ash oil.

{{ikon:damla}}

Hydraulic oil

Anti-wear hydraulic oil that feeds the pumps, valves, cylinders and hydraulic motors. The viscosity is chosen to suit the machine and the climate.

{{ikon:sanziman}}

Transmission and torque converter oil

Power transmission fluid for powershift, automatic and shuttle transmissions. It must be compatible with wet clutches and brakes.

{{ikon:disli}}

Final drive and swing reducer oil

On tracked machines, the left and right final drives and the swing reducer are each filled separately. Depending on the brand, this is a gear oil or a power transmission fluid.

{{ikon:gosterge}}

Axle, differential and wet brake oil

For the axles of wheel loaders, backhoe loaders and wheeled excavators. Ordinary gear oil must not be used in axles with wet brakes; the brakes will slip and squeal.

{{ikon:kalkan}}

Grease, antifreeze, AdBlue

Grease for pivot points and chisel paste for breaker tools; long-life antifreeze and AdBlue for newer engines.

Tracked machines

What is a final drive?

The final drive is the last unit in the drivetrain and turns the track; in Turkey it is called “cer”, but the English term final drive is widely used in the trade as well. It houses the travel motor and a planetary reduction gear. This is where the machine's highest torque is produced.

The toothed wheel on the outside of the final drive that engages the track chain is called the sprocket. The swing reducer is not a final drive; it is a separate unit that turns the upper structure.

  • Complete final drives and internal parts: planetary gears, sun gear, bearings, floating seals
  • Travel motors and seal kits
  • Sprockets (segmented or one-piece)
  • Separate final drive oil for the left and right final drives

Frequently asked questions

About parts and oils.

Which brands do you find parts for?

We work with every make and model. Send us the machine's make, model and serial number; we research the part on that basis and give you a price. We are not an authorised dealer of any brand.

Genuine or aftermarket?

You can ask for a price for either option. Our quote states clearly whether the part is genuine or aftermarket.

Which oil does my machine take?

The grade and viscosity of the oil are given in the machine's operator's manual and vary by make, model and year. If you do not have the manual to hand, send us the make, model and serial number and we will work out the right oil together.

Can engine oil be used in the hydraulic system?

Only if the manufacturer specifies it in the manual. Some older machines use engine oil in the hydraulics, most newer machines do not. An oil not listed in the manual can damage the seals and the pump; do not mix different types of oil.

When should the final drive oil be checked?

The change and inspection intervals are given in the manual in operating hours. Oil loss from the final drive or metal particles in the oil can indicate that the internal parts are worn; early action can avoid replacing the whole final drive.

Do you fit the parts?

You can buy the part only, or we can fit it in the workshop or on site during working hours.
