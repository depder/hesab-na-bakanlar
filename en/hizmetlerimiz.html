Our Services

Solutions for hydraulic, transmission and diesel engine faults on excavators, wheel loaders, backhoe loaders, cranes and other construction machinery — in the workshop and on site.

Core specialisms

The systems that carry the machine's power.

Core specialism01

Hydraulic System Service

We diagnose hydraulic faults such as loss of power, slow operation, leaks and overheating, and repair and maintain the entire hydraulic circuit, from pump to cylinder.

  • Hydraulic pump and hydraulic motor faults
  • Control valve and hydraulic circuit faults
  • Hydraulic cylinder overhaul: seals, rods, barrels
  • Track travel motor, final drive and swing problems
  • Hose, fitting and leak repair; scheduled oil and filter service

Frequently asked questions

What you may want to know before asking.

Which brands do you work on?

We work on the hydraulic systems, transmissions, engines, buckets and mechanical components of construction machinery of every brand. We are not an authorised service centre of any brand.

What are the signs of a hydraulic fault?

Slow operation, loss of power, overheating of the hydraulic oil, leaks, whining or humming from the pump and cylinders drifting down on their own can all point to a hydraulic fault. Early diagnosis keeps the fault from getting worse.

When does an engine need an overhaul?

A marked loss of power, high oil consumption, heavy smoke or hard starting can indicate that an overhaul is needed. The final decision is made after the engine has been diagnosed.

What is the difference between on-site service hours and workshop hours?

Workshop hours: Mon–Fri 08:30–18:00 · Sat 08:30–13:30 · Closed on Sunday. On-site service is provided during the same working hours.

How long does a repair or overhaul take?

It depends on the type of fault, the extent of the damage and how long the parts take to arrive. After diagnosis we tell you the estimated time.

Can I send a service request outside working hours?

Yes. Requests received outside workshop hours are answered at the start of the next working day.
